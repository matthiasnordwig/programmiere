**Visitors don't see it.** No image grids, no "I'm not a robot" box, nothing to click. When they
press *Send*, their browser solves a small cryptographic task in a few milliseconds. People don't
notice. Spam bots either fail or have to spend so much computing power per message that mass spam
no longer pays off.

**Any form, not just a list of integrations.** Logins, registrations, comments, WooCommerce checkout
and reviews, Contact Form 7, Elementor, WPForms, Gravity Forms, Ninja Forms, Fluent Forms,
Formidable and dozens more. The plugin recognises a submission by its signature instead of hooking
into each form plugin. So a hand-coded form or a rare form builder can be added in under a minute:
submit it once in analysis mode, then click save.

**Nothing leaves your server.** No account, no API keys, no third-party scripts, nothing to add to
the cookie banner. There is no outside service that can go down and break your forms, and none that
slows the page down.

## Why not reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha, Turnstile or Akismet?

They need an account and keys, load scripts from other people's servers or send your visitors'
messages elsewhere for analysis, and when in doubt they still make real people solve a challenge.
This plugin does none of that.
